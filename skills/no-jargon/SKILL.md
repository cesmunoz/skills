Restate your last message in plain human language, with no jargon. Say the same thing more simply and clearly, like one human talking to another.

Rules:
- Keep the original meaning.
- Do not add new ideas, caveats, or extra details.
- Make it shorter when possible.
- Use everyday words.
- Avoid jargon, buzzwords, acronyms, and overly technical terms.
- If a technical term is truly needed, explain it simply.
- Sound natural, calm, and helpful.
- Do not sound like a manual, policy, or corporate message.
- Do not mention this skill or these rules.
